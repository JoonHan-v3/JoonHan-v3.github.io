/About Me

I am currently pursuing a Master’s degree in Robotics at the University of Michigan, driven by a passion for robotics, artificial intelligence, and autonomous systems. My goal is to develop physical AI systems that can understand and communicate with people, reason about their environment, and act at a human-like level. ...more

My interest in intelligent machines began in my teenage years, inspired by Transformers and the idea of robots that can perceive, think, and act autonomously. This led me to study Computer Science at the University of Michigan, where I focused on the software side of autonomous systems. During my undergraduate studies, I built a strong foundation in AI through coursework in Machine Learning, Computer Vision, Reinforcement Learning, and Artificial Intelligence, along with research and professional certifications that strengthened my practical skills.

After graduation, I joined the Robotics Master’s program to expand from intelligent software to the physical systems that enable autonomy. My current work focuses on perception, planning, control, localization, and autonomous navigation.

By combining AI and robotics, I aim to build intelligent systems that can perceive, reason, interact with humans, and act meaningfully in the real world.

Robotics Lawn Mower — Version 1

First version of an autonomous lawn mower robot. Modeled the base platform with URDF and Xacro and simulated it in Gazebo, with differential-drive wheel control, collision geometry, inertia, odometry, and joint states. This is the physical foundation for future versions with autonomous navigation and obstacle avoidance.
